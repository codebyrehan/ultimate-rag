# Annual Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance and is subject to manager approval.
Unused leave rolls over for up to 10 days into the next calendar year.
Annual leave does not accrue during unpaid leave periods.

# Sick Leave Policy

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year, accrued monthly at 0.83 days.
Sick leave cannot be carried over; it expires at the end of the calendar year.
Medical certification is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Sick leave is separate from annual leave and does not reduce vacation entitlement.

# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with prior manager approval.
Remote work requests must be submitted at least 48 hours in advance.
Equipment and internet reimbursement are provided for full-time remote workers.
The company provides a laptop, monitor, and ergonomic accessories for all remote setup.

# Performance Review

Performance is evaluated annually through a structured review process involving
self-assessment, peer feedback, and direct manager evaluation.
Reviews cover three pillars: technical contribution, collaboration, and leadership.
Scores range from 1 (needs improvement) to 5 (exceeds expectations).
Calibration meetings ensure consistent scoring across teams and managers.

# Employee Benefits

The company offers comprehensive benefits including medical, dental, and vision insurance.
A 401k plan with a 4% employer match is available to all full-time employees.
Life insurance coverage of 2x annual salary is provided at no cost to employees.
Flexible spending accounts (FSA) are available for healthcare and dependent care.
The company also provides professional development stipends of $3,000 annually.

# Resignation Policy

Employees are expected to provide at least 2 weeks' written notice before resignation.
For senior roles, a 4-week notice period applies.
Final pay will be issued on the last regular pay cycle, including any accrued but unused leave.
The employee must return all company property including laptops, access cards, and keys.
